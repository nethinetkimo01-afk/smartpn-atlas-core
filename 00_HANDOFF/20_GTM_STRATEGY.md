## The Core Narrative Logic (Fixed Structure)

This logic chain applies to ALL outreach, regardless of audience.
The entry point changes. The logic does not.

Step 1: Start with what THEY care about
Step 2: Show a problem they have not fully considered
Step 3: Introduce the hidden risk behind the obvious solution
Step 4: Explain why the industry is stuck (Standard Zero Zone)
Step 5: Position SmartPN Atlas as the governance layer that makes it work
Step 6: Close with: Shared Language + Governance Layer = the only scalable solution

Example using Matt Priest (tariff / production transfer angle):

Entry: Brand transferring production due to tariffs needs fast sampling in new factory.
Problem: With Shared BOM (same material code/name across brand/factory/supplier),
         each material has a barcode. New factory can sample correctly 100% of the time.
Hidden risk: Shared BOM means 100% open data. Completely against commercial logic.
             This is exactly why manufacturing is still in the Standard Zero Zone.
Standard Zero Zone: same material has different codes and names across brand/factory/supplier.
SmartPN position: Governance Layer that manages the barcode.
                  Data is confidential. Only authorized parties see core data.
                  SmartPN does not store customer data (no central system).
Conclusion: Shared Language is the right direction.
            Shared Language + Governance Layer = the only solution that can scale
            in manufacturing-side supply chains.

[Target] Name / Title / Organization
[Recent Focus] One sentence summary of what they care about now
[Summary] 2-3 sentences. What is this person thinking about?
[Key Paragraph] STAR MARK the most relevant content with direct quote or paraphrase
[SmartPN Entry Point] Based on their focus, where do we enter the conversation?

This format applies to:
- Manual research (now)
- Automated research (when method is confirmed working)

## Outreach Rules

1. Research first. Find their recent content. Identify shared concern.
2. Never claim expertise we do not have (DPP execution, ESG compliance, GS1).
3. Enter from THEIR concern, not from our product.
4. Use the core narrative logic structure every time.
5. Each person gets a different entry point. Same logic. Different door.
6. Draft only after reading their content. Never draft blind.

## Core Philosophy (Apply Everywhere)

Title = Problem + Solution. One glance. No explanation needed.

Even someone who did not attend the presentation should:
- Identify whether they have this problem
- Understand how SmartPN Atlas solves it

This applies to:
- Every Scenario PPT title
- Every LinkedIn image headline
- Every Demo software function label
- Every website page

---

## 1. Fixed Layout Rule

Every Scenario slide follows this exact structure:

`
[Product image: shoe]    [Brand BOM ??center top, shared]    [Product image: apparel]

CURRENT ??Independent BOM          |          AFTER SMARTPN ATLAS ??Shared BOM
                                   |
Factory A (distinct background)    |    Factory A (distinct background)
Factory B (different background)   |    Factory B (different background)
`

- Left side: CURRENT
- Right side: AFTER SMARTPN ATLAS
- Center top: Brand BOM (shown ONCE if identical on both sides ??never repeat)
- Two product images: shoe (left) + apparel (right) = applies to both industries

---

## 2. Product Image Rule

Every slide shows TWO product images: shoe + apparel.
Purpose: signal that this solution applies to BOTH footwear AND apparel industry.
NOT to represent different product lines.
Position: flanking the Brand BOM at the top center.

---

## 3. Brand BOM Rule

If Brand BOM is identical on both Current and After sides:
- Show it ONCE in the center top
- Do NOT repeat it on left and right
- Reduces information, increases focus

---

## 4. Color Rule (CRITICAL)

Colors guide the customer's eye. Different color = different thing. Same color = same thing.

Rules:
- Factory A and Factory B must have DIFFERENT background colors (they are independent factories)
- Brand BOM has its own consistent style
- CURRENT section header: RED background, white text (signals problem)
- AFTER SMARTPN ATLAS section header: GREEN background (signals solution)
- Items needing attention: RED text / RED background (change-code, change-name, ?, .90)
- Normal items: follow design system (white background, dark text)
- NEVER use different colors for the same type of element
- NEVER use same color for different types of elements

---

## 5. Information Minimalism Rule

Customer brain is for THINKING, not for reading.

Rules:
- Show the minimum information needed to make the point
- If two sides show identical content, show it once
- Use generic labels (brand-code, brand-name, fac-A-code) not real material names
- Real material names (mesh, TPU, foam) = noise for CEO audience
- Use change-code / change-name to directly signal "this changed" ??customer brain immediately asks: "who changed it? did I know?"
- Fewer elements = more focused attention

---

## 6. Animation Rule

One sentence you say = one element appears on screen.

Sequence for S02:
1. Product appears (shoe + apparel)
2. Brand BOM appears ??"You provided BOM to your factory"
3. Factory A Current appears ??"But you cannot confirm they used the same material"
4. Factory B Current appears ??"Two factories, even more confusion"
5. Factory A After appears ??"After SmartPN Atlas, same code, same name, confirmed same material"
6. Factory B After appears ??"Each factory's material, price difference, all visible"
7. change-code / .90 highlighted ??"Transparent supply chain. Hidden profit revealed."

Animation rule: Never show everything at once. Reveal step by step matching your speech.

---

## 7. Section Header Rule

CURRENT section:
- Label: "CURRENT ??Independent BOM" (or relevant description)
- Style: RED background, white text, ALL CAPS
- Purpose: signal the problem state

AFTER SMARTPN ATLAS section:
- Label: "AFTER SMARTPN ATLAS ??Shared BOM" (or relevant description)
- Style: GREEN background, dark text, ALL CAPS
- "Shared BOM" or key concept underlined
- Purpose: signal the solution state

---

## 8. Factory Label Rule

Factory labels (Factory A, Factory B) appear on the LEFT SIDE of each table row, vertically centered.
Factory A and Factory B use DIFFERENT background colors.
They are independent companies ??visual must reflect this.

---

## 9. Title Rule

Slide title = direct, clear, no jargon.
Format: "S02 ??Shared Language and Shared BOM"
Left accent bar: Taupe #54463A, 3px wide vertical line before title text.

Titles must work standalone:
- Person who never attended can read the title and understand the problem
- Person who never attended can look at the slide and understand the solution
- No hidden meaning, no insider language

---

## 10. Scenario Tag Rule

Bottom right of every slide:
"SCENARIO XX / SMARTPN ATLAS"
Font: 7pt, light gray
Purpose: identify slide in a stack, maintain brand consistency

---

## 11. Demo and LinkedIn Alignment Rule

PPT design language must match Demo software and LinkedIn images:
- Same color system
- Same terminology (brand-code, brand-name, change-code)
- Same mock data numbers
- Customer sees PPT ??opens Demo ??same visual language ??zero unfamiliarity

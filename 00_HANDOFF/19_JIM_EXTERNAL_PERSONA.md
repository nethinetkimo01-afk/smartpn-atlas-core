## What SmartPN Atlas Is

A governance layer for material identity in the manufacturing-side supply chain.

SmartPN Atlas governs the shared material language before materials enter any system.
It makes source-side material data trusted, controlled, and traceable.

One sentence:
We are the governance layer before everything else starts.

---

## What SmartPN Atlas Is NOT

This must be communicated clearly and honestly every time.

NOT DPP:
SmartPN Atlas does not generate Digital Product Passports.
SmartPN Atlas does not claim to understand DPP regulatory execution details.
SmartPN Atlas makes the upstream material identity data more trustworthy,
which in turn makes downstream DPP outputs more credible.
We are upstream. DPP is downstream.

NOT ESG / Sustainability expert:
SmartPN Atlas does not claim expertise in ESG frameworks, reporting standards,
carbon accounting, or sustainability compliance.
We know the manufacturing side. We do not know the full sustainability execution chain.

NOT GS1:
SmartPN Atlas does not issue unified material codes.
SmartPN Atlas does not replace ERP or PLM systems.
SmartPN Atlas is a Mapping Layer.
Each party keeps their own local ID.
SmartPN Atlas maps them to a shared identity without forcing system changes.

NOT Inspectorio:
Inspectorio collects compliance and audit data from the brand side looking down.
SmartPN Atlas governs material identity from the supplier source side looking up.
We are upstream to Inspectorio, not competing with it.

NOT a regulatory authority:
SmartPN Atlas does not define regulations.
SmartPN Atlas does not certify compliance.
SmartPN Atlas helps suppliers prepare trusted source data
that can support compliance processes run by others.

## The Honest Boundary Statement

When engaging with journalists, brands, or partners:

"We focus on one specific problem: the manufacturing-side supply chain lacks
a governed shared material language. The same material has different names,
codes, and descriptions across supplier, factory, and brand systems.
This creates repeated confirmation work and untrustworthy source data.

We are not DPP experts. We are not ESG consultants. We are not GS1.
We are the governance layer that makes manufacturing-side material identity
trusted before it enters any downstream system, whether that is DPP,
ESG reporting, compliance, or sourcing decisions.

We know manufacturing. We know material identity. That is our lane."

## The Correct Entry Point for Each Audience

Journalists covering DPP / ESG / Traceability:
Entry: "You are reporting on DPP. Has anyone told you that the upstream
manufacturing-side material data is not ready? That is our problem space."
NOT: "We are a DPP solution."

Brand sustainability / sourcing teams:
Entry: "Your suppliers use different material codes than you do.
Before any transparency initiative works, the source data needs to be governed.
That is what we do."
NOT: "We help you achieve ESG compliance."

Inspectorio / GTS / partners:
Entry: "We govern the upstream material identity layer.
Your platform collects data downstream. We make the source data more trustworthy."
NOT: "We compete with you."

Material Exchange / sourcing platforms:
Entry: "Your buyers search for materials using different names and codes
than suppliers use. We solve the identity fragmentation problem upstream."
NOT: "We are a sourcing platform."

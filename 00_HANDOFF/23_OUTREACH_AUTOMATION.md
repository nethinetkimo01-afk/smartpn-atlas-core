# SmartPN Atlas ??Outreach Reader Interface
Version: v1.0 | 2026-06-01
Status: CONFIRMED. UI locked. Backend (Google Sheet write-back) pending.

---

## Purpose

Daily interface for Jim to review outreach targets produced by Make automation.
Jim reads, edits draft, decides to send or skip.
Pulls data from Google Sheet populated by Make at 08:00 daily.

---

## Field Order (CONFIRMED)

Target ??Source Article + Argument ??Key Finding ??Entry Point ??Contact Channel ??Draft ??Status

---

## Field Definitions

Target:
- Name
- Organization
- Type (journalist / brand / platform / analyst)

Source Article + Argument:
- Article title + URL
- Why this article is the entry point

Key Finding:
- Most relevant finding, marked clearly
- What in their content connects to SmartPN Atlas

Entry Point:
- How SmartPN Atlas enters their world
- Based on their language, not ours

Contact Channel:
- Email: mailto link (opens mail app)
- LinkedIn: opens profile page
- Website: opens URL
- RULE: Button only appears if it executes a real action. No fake buttons.

Draft:
- Large text area
- Jim can edit directly
- Follows 22_WRITING_RULES.md logic chain

Status:
- Draft
- Sent
- Skip

---

## Draft Controls (ALL 3 ALWAYS VISIBLE)

Edit ??toggles editing mode, repeatable
Save ??preserves content while staying in edit mode
Upload ??confirms final version for write-back to Google Sheet

RULE: Upload is the ONLY accepted placeholder button.
All other buttons must execute a real action.
Upload is pending backend Google Sheet connection.

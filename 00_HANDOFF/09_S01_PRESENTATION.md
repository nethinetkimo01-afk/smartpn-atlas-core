## Jim Presentation Script (confirmed)

After SmartPN Atlas, you get a material library that is larger, more current, more accurate, and permission-controlled.

Assume your current library has 2 items. In SmartPN Atlas, you see 4 items. Why?

2 items are new ??added Today. Before, your team collected data offline. Now, Supplier maintains their own data. This reduces internal work and improves accuracy, because Supplier is responsible for their own data quality.

The material code and name in SmartPN Atlas are shared across the manufacturing-side supply chain. So Brand, Factory, and Supplier now have a Shared Language.

Everyone wants to protect their material library. In permission control, you can see 4 items ??but others can only see 1. Because only 1 item is set to OPEN.

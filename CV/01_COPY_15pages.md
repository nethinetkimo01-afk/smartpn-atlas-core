### P9 ｜Before/After · D03（左右對比）
- **標題：** Open data doesn't move. Permissioned data does.
- **橘金重點：** `permission`
- **正文：**
  - **Left（推不動）：** When data is simply made public, no one adopts it — suppliers won't expose what they can't control.
  - **Right（推得動）：** When data carries permission — the owner decides who sees what — it can finally spread.
  SmartPN Atlas is not a central database. Each supplier keeps data in their own system; SmartPN governs identity and permission so the right data reaches the right party, and never leaves the owner.
- **圖示（GPT）：** D03 左右對比。左＝公開但卡住，右＝權限且流動。

### P10 ｜Before/After · D03（左右對比）
- **標題：** From a burden into an opportunity
- **橘金重點：** `opportunity`
- **正文：**
  - **Before（負擔）：** Suppliers filled in data only when a brand demanded it. Data was a cost.
  - **After（商機）：** Now a brand can search and find them. The supplier is seen. Permission decides which brand gets what. Data becomes a way to win business — so the supplier maintains it actively, on their own.
- **圖示（GPT）：** D03 左右對比。

### P11 ｜概念圖型 · D04

  GS1 manages codes. PLM manages a brand's own data. Traceability platforms assume the source is already trustworthy. No one governs identity that is shared and permissioned across brand, OEM, and supplier — on the manufacturing side. That layer is the gap. SmartPN Atlas fills it.

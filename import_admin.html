DS-02 · Import FOB Price List

Upload the FOB Price List Excel file (system export).
Required: Article # and at least two of: CuttingStitching StockfittingAssembly LC Total
Also mapped: Model #, Model Name, Factory, Season, Category, Stage, Valid From, LC CTB.
Dedup key: Article #. Changed fields are logged to change_log.

DS-01 · Import Season Plan (SP)

Upload the Season Plan Excel export (~6 383 rows × 76 cols).
Required columns: Article ID Product Type DESC Calendar Month
Quantity column: Total (or Quantity / Qty).
All 76 columns are stored as raw JSON. Dedup key: ART + Type + Month.
